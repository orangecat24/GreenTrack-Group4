Lighting choices and electricity use
Source: ENERGY STAR, Learn About Brightness
https://www.energystar.gov/products/learn-about-brightness

Choose the brightness you need in lumens, then compare the wattage of bulbs
with similar light output. A lower-wattage bulb draws less power while on.
Changing a bulb you use for many hours usually saves more energy than making
the same switch in a rarely used fixture. Check that the replacement works
with the fixture and any dimmer.
