Laundry habits and electricity use
Source: ENERGY STAR, Clothes Washers
https://www.energystar.gov/products/clothes_washers

Use a cold wash for laundry that can be cleaned at that temperature, following
its care labels. This can reduce the energy used to heat water. Run full loads
within the washer's instructions. Air-dry suitable laundry when practical to
reduce dryer use.
